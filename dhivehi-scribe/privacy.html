Dhivehi Scribe Privacy Policy

Publisher: SimpleworldApps · Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy describes the Windows version of Dhivehi Scribe, a local app that converts Dhivehi speech and printed images into editable Thaana text.

Audio, images and documents stay on your computer

The app processes microphone audio, audio files and images using the models included with the installation or OCR models you explicitly connect from a local folder. Audio, images, transcripts and file contents are not sent to SimpleworldApps or a cloud transcription or OCR provider. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics or automatic developer crash-reporting service. Your inputs and edits are not collected for automatic model training.

Microphone and selected files

Microphone access is used when you choose Start listening and allow access in Windows. Stop listening releases the microphone. Pause pauses capture. You can revoke microphone access in Windows Settings. Only files you select or drop for transcription, image reading or backup import, and model folders you connect, are read.

Image paste reads the image supplied by your clipboard when you press Ctrl+V in the image panel. Copy writes the document text to your clipboard when you request it. The app does not monitor the clipboard in the background.

Recordings and imported audio are held in memory while used. They are not automatically saved as audio files by the app. Choosing Save recording creates a file you control. Closing the app or replacing a recording releases its in-memory copy. Your original audio files remain where you stored them.

Local storage and retention

Document text, titles, text-size preferences, model settings and OCR source metadata (source filename, selected model, extraction time and quality mode) are saved in the app's local Windows profile. Image pixels are held in memory and are not included in saved documents or JSON backups. Data remains until you remove it or the application data. Clear empties the current text and offers a temporary Undo option. Selecting a new audio file creates a new document and preserves the previous saved draft. Export text saves a text file; Transcript backup exports a JSON file containing saved documents and OCR source metadata. Imported backups are merged with existing drafts.

Help → Open app data folder shows the app's exact data location. To remove all local saved data, first export anything you need, close the app, and remove that data folder. Windows uninstall behavior can vary by installation type; export important drafts before uninstalling. We cannot retrieve or delete files on your computer remotely.

Security and sharing

The local speech service listens only on this computer and uses an app-generated access token. Transcripts and exports are not separately encrypted by Dhivehi Scribe; access depends on your Windows account, device protection and any folder permissions you choose. People or software with access to your account or exported files may be able to read them.

Copying text, exporting files or saving a recording puts that information in locations you choose. Any later sharing is under your control. Obtain the necessary permission before recording another person.

Logs and external services

Local diagnostic logs record engine startup and errors, which may include local model paths. The app does not intentionally log recognized text, audio or image pixels. Logs are kept in the app data folder and are not automatically sent to the publisher.

Optional links to model information and this policy open your browser. Those websites follow their own privacy practices. Microsoft handles Store downloads, updates, ratings and Store diagnostics under Microsoft's privacy policy. The app's core transcription functions work offline after installation.

Support and policy updates

If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send. Do not send private recordings or transcripts unless necessary for your request. We use support messages to respond and investigate the reported issue.
