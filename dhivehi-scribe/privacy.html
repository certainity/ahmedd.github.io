Dhivehi Scribe Privacy Policy

Publisher: SimpleworldApps · Updated: 22 October 2026

Dhivehi Scribe converts Dhivehi speech and printed images into editable Thaana text. This policy covers the Windows app. There are two editions: the original local edition and the smaller online edition. The online edition is clearly marked “Free online recognition” in the app and asks before sending each file. Installing an update does not automatically upload existing documents or recordings.

Original local edition

Local editions process audio and images using models on your computer: included models, optional models you explicitly download, or OCR models you connect from a local folder. Audio, images, transcripts and file contents are not sent to SimpleworldApps or a cloud recognition provider. Where available, optional model downloads connect to GitHub; GitHub receives connection information such as your IP address and requested model URL, but no recognition inputs. Recognition works offline when the required models are installed.

Online edition: your choice before each upload

Choosing a file, pasting an image or recording with the microphone does not upload that content. Select Review and send to see the file, purpose, service limits and retention information. The file is sent only after you consent and select Send and transcribe or Send and extract text. You can cancel before sending. The online edition requires internet access and depends on the availability of SimpleworldApps’ processing computer.

After confirmation, the app encrypts the selected audio or image and sends the encrypted bytes through Cloudflare to SimpleworldApps’ processing computer. That computer decrypts the file, runs our speech or OCR model, and returns an encrypted text result. The app decrypts the result and saves the completed text on your device. Encryption keys are exchanged through the service over HTTPS; this design still relies on the app, service operator and Cloudflare infrastructure being trustworthy.

The online request contains the media type, encrypted size, encrypted media, an encrypted per-request key, random request credentials and a verification token. The original filename is not included in that request. The service does not request your existing saved documents, other files, address book or model files.

Processing and retention

Plaintext audio, images and recognized text are processed in memory on the processing computer. They are not intentionally written to media files, included in service logs or used for model training. Memory is released after processing; immediate secure erasure of every memory copy is not guaranteed. The processing container is removed after each job.

Encrypted uploads expire after 15 minutes. Their chunks are removed after completion, failure or cancellation, or by periodic cleanup after expiry. Encrypted results expire after one hour and the app requests deletion after retrieval or cancellation. A disconnected app cannot guarantee immediate deletion, so expiry remains the fallback. Cleanup runs approximately every 10 minutes.

Request metadata, including time, kind, size, state and a daily salted hash of the connecting IP address, is retained for up to two days to enforce free usage limits and operate the queue. The broker does not intentionally store the raw IP address in its request database. Cloudflare’s Free-plan D1 recovery backups can retain encrypted data and metadata for up to seven days. Deletion from the active database does not immediately remove those recovery copies. The processing computer’s private session key is held in memory and rotated; ordinary operation does not archive it.

Cloudflare and other providers

Cloudflare provides the public connection, encrypted queue and Turnstile anti-abuse verification. It receives network information, including your IP address, request details and browser/device information used by verification. Opening the confirmation dialog loads the verification service, even if you later cancel without sending a file. Cloudflare may process connection information in other countries under its own policies. Read Cloudflare’s privacy policy and Turnstile’s privacy policy.

The app has no advertising, analytics or automatic developer crash-reporting service. Inputs, results and edits are not automatically collected for training. Microsoft handles Store downloads, updates and Store diagnostics under Microsoft’s privacy statement. Optional website links open in your browser and follow the destination’s policies.

Google sign-in and the free daily allowance

Windows editions that show “Sign in with Google” use Google to verify your identity and email address. Sign-in opens your system browser and requests only basic identity and email (openid and email). It does not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts or your recordings. Your Google password is entered only on Google’s page. Google processes sign-in under Google’s privacy policy.

The app exchanges a one-time sign-in code through our Cloudflare service. That service verifies Google’s signed identity response, uses the email address to identify the signed-in account to you, and keeps a salted hash of the Google account identifier for sessions and daily usage. It does not store your email, Google identity token or Google access token in its database. The Windows app stores the email and a separate Scribe session credential protected by Windows account encryption. Sessions expire after seven days. Sign out revokes that session and removes the app’s saved sign-in credential.

Signed-in accounts receive up to 20 recognitions a day, shared between audio and image requests. An unfinished request temporarily reserves one use; failed, cancelled or expired unfinished requests release it. Successful requests remain counted even after their encrypted content is deleted. The allowance resets at midnight UTC, shown in your local time in the app. Shared processing capacity and availability can still limit requests. Earlier editions without account controls continue to use their existing connection-based limits during the update rollout.

Account session records contain only a credential hash, salted account identifier and creation/expiry times. Expired records are removed by periodic cleanup. One-time sign-in challenges expire after ten minutes and their anti-abuse metadata is kept for up to one day. Daily usage metadata follows the two-day retention described above; recovery backups can retain deleted records for up to seven days. Signing out does not erase completed daily usage or your locally saved documents. Google identity information is used only for sign-in, account security and the free allowance; it is not used for advertising or model training. Our use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements where applicable.

Microphone, selected files and clipboard

The app uses the microphone only after you start recording and grant access. Stop recording releases it; Pause pauses capture. Revoke permission through Windows Settings. Recordings and selected files are held in memory while used and are not automatically saved as media files. Replacing them or closing the app releases the app’s in-memory copy. Original input files remain in their original locations.

Image paste reads the image supplied by the clipboard when you press Ctrl+V in Image to text. Copy writes your document text when requested. The app does not monitor the clipboard in the background. Only selected files, pasted images and, in local editions, connected model folders are read.

Documents saved on your device

Document text, titles, text-size preferences and, where available, OCR source metadata are stored in the app’s local Windows profile. Image pixels and audio are not included in document backups. Clear empties the current text and offers temporary Undo. Choosing a new input starts a new document and preserves earlier drafts. JSON backup import adds documents without replacing existing drafts.

Local documents and exports are not separately encrypted by Dhivehi Scribe. Protect them using your Windows account and device security. Export important drafts before uninstalling or clearing application data. Help → Open app data folder shows the exact local profile. To remove saved local data, export anything needed, close the app and remove that folder. SimpleworldApps cannot retrieve or delete your local files remotely.

Your choices and support

You may keep editing saved documents without sending new files. Only send recordings and images you are permitted to process, and obtain necessary permission before recording others. If you contact support, we receive the email address and information you choose to send and use it to respond and investigate the issue. Do not email private recordings unless they are necessary for your request.
